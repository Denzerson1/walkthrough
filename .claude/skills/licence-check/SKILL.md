---
name: licence-check
description: Use before adding any dependency, ML model, dataset, texture or 3D asset
---
Before adding $ARGUMENTS:
1. Find the licence at the official source (LICENSE file, model card or asset page).
2. Allowed: MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, CC0, or a paid licence that permits commercial use. If it is non-commercial or unclear, stop and ask me.
3. Add a row to docs/LICENSES.md: name, version, licence, link, how we use it.
